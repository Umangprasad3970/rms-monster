Erection & Commissioning

The critical phases of a wind power project.

The erection and commissioning of wind turbines involves installing components, integrating them into the electrical system, and ensuring the turbines are fully operational.

Site Preparation

Preparing access roads, laydown areas, and the working platform for heavy equipment.

Delivery of Components

Coordinated transport and staging of turbines, blades, towers and nacelles to site.

Foundation Installation

Engineered foundations built to carry turbine loads under all operating conditions.

Tower Erection

Sectional tower assembly using cranes rated for the turbine class.

Nacelle Installation

Lifting and securing the nacelle housing the drivetrain and generator.

Rotor Blade Installation

Precision blade fitting to complete the rotor assembly.

Electrical & Mechanical Components

Integrating control systems, cabling and mechanical subsystems.

Safety Inspection & Testing

Verifying installation quality against safety and engineering standards.

Commissioning Tests

Functional testing to confirm the turbine performs as designed.

Grid Connection

Connecting the turbine into the electrical grid.

Handover & Operations

Handover to the operations team for ongoing power generation.
