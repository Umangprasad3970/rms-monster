02 · Solar Power Plant Projects

Generating electricity — and heat — from sunlight.

A solar project involves the installation, development and utilization of solar energy systems, ranging in scale from small residential installations to large utility-scale solar farms.

Residential Commercial & Industrial Utility-Scale
Types of Solar Projects

Sized to the site, not the other way around.

Solar projects have gained popularity due to the decreasing cost of solar panels and increasing awareness of environmental sustainability, supported by government incentives and subsidies.

A

Residential Solar

Solar panels installed on homes to provide electricity for personal use.

B

Commercial & Industrial Solar

Larger-scale projects for businesses, factories, and other commercial enterprises.

C

Utility-Scale Solar

Massive solar farms that generate electricity for the grid and can power thousands of homes.
