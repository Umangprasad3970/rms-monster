Complementary Dynamics

Maximizing transmission capacity & land footprint.

Solar arrays produce power during peak daytime sun, while wind turbine generation frequently peaks in late afternoon, evening, and monsoon seasons. Combining both profiles on a shared high-voltage evacuation corridor optimizes transmission line asset utilization up to 45% CUF.
