Who We Are

Our unwavering commitment revolves around effecting a positive change in our environment.

Our primary objective is to lead the charge towards a sustainable, clean energy future, delivering innovative, reliable and cost-effective green energy solutions. We are diligently working towards the realization of India's ambitious target of achieving 500 GW of renewable energy by 2030, with a particular emphasis on decarbonizing carbon emissions.

In this pursuit, we have forged partnerships with industry leaders well-versed in the renewable energy sector, aiding them in their quest for achieving net-zero goals.

We proudly introduce ourselves as a proficient team, boasting extensive expertise in the installation of Wind Energy Generators, Solar Power Plants, and their seamless commissioning. Moreover, we are actively venturing into the emerging realm of EV Charging Stations, poised for robust growth alongside the surging electric vehicle market.

At Neoserve, we remain resolutely committed to delivering the utmost quality and safety standards in the execution of projects spanning Wind, Solar, Hybrid systems, and various service offerings. Guided by a team of astute and visionary leaders, we possess exceptional skills in handling heavy installation operations — particularly within the Wind industry, Solar Power Projects, PEB structures, EV Charging stations & Green Hydrogen Plants.

Vision & Roadmap

From installation partner to full EPC company.

Currently, Neoserve Projects is involved in Wind Turbine, Solar and PEB Building manpower supply and installation services. Over the next three years, we aim to evolve into a comprehensive EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) company.
