04 · EV Charging Stations

Installed and commissioned to run safely, from day one.

The installation and commissioning of an Electric Vehicle (EV) charging station is a critical process to ensure it functions safely and efficiently — and stays that way through ongoing maintenance and monitoring.

Level 1 AC Level 2 AC DC Fast Charging
Key Steps & Considerations

From site survey to live charging.

Site Selection & Planning

Choosing a suitable location — visibility, accessibility, proximity to power sources, and potential demand — and evaluating whether the electrical infrastructure can support the station's power requirements.

Regulatory Compliance

Checking local and national regulations and codes for charging station installation, including electrical codes, building permits and safety standards, and obtaining necessary permits.

Electrical Infrastructure

Ensuring the electrical infrastructure can provide the required power, upgrading it if necessary, and determining whether the station is grid-tied or uses a dedicated power source such as solar.

Charging Station Selection

Choosing the appropriate charger type for the intended use — Level 1 (AC), Level 2 (AC), or DC fast chargers — compatible with the vehicles that will use it.

Installation

Installing the charging station per manufacturer guidelines and safety standards, with all electrical connections properly made and safety features tested.

Network Connectivity

Establishing network connectivity for remote monitoring, management and payment processing, and configuring software and communication protocols.

Safety Features

Verifying that ground fault protection and emergency shutoffs are functional, with proper signage and safety instructions in place.

Commissioning

Testing and verifying the station is fully functional and meets safety and performance standards, including electrical and communication checks.

Ongoing Operations

Keeping a station running, not just switched on.

Regularly updating the station's software and firmware is crucial to keep up with technological advancements and ensure compatibility with new EV models.
